Keeping it current

This page is rebuilt by tools/build-snapshot.py from extracts in .local/qbo/. Neither the extracts nor this file are tracked by git — .local/ is ignored in full, so real book data never reaches the repository.

To refresh, ask Claude in a session connected to QuickBooks. The pull goes through the QuickBooks connector rather than the script, because it needs OAuth credentials the script deliberately does not hold. Once qbo-local can authenticate on its own, it replaces this step and the data stops being a snapshot at all.

Figures are as pulled and drift from QuickBooks the moment anything is entered there. Where the two disagree, QuickBooks is right — it is the book of record.
